Phone notifications (web push) for the installed app
----------------------------------------------------
Turn on (one time):
  1. go run ./cmd/vapid-keys           prints VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY / VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY
  2. Add both on Render (Environment), plus VAPID_SUBJECT=mailto:you@yourdomain.com
     Keep the pair for good. Changing it makes every phone subscribe again.
  3. Redeploy. The log says "Push notifications are on".
  Without the keys the feature is simply off; nothing else changes.

What people see:
  - After installing the app and opening it from the home screen (logged in), a card offers
    "Get order updates on your phone". Tapping "Turn on" shows the phone's own permission question.
    (iPhone needs iOS 16.4+, and the site added to the Home Screen first.)
  - Right after they allow it, a real test notification is sent.
  - Account > Phone notifications: turn on/off and "Send a test".
  - Tapping a notification opens the right page (orders, messages...).

What triggers a notification: every in-app notification (order paid/confirmed/status changes,
rider updates, vendor "new order", scheduled orders, group orders, wallet) and every new chat
message to the other person.

Files: push/ (encryption RFC 8291 + VAPID RFC 8292, standard library only), handlers/push.go,
migration 0005_push_subscriptions.sql, static/sw.js, static/js/push.js, static/css/push.css.
Routes: GET /push/key, GET /push/status, POST /push/subscribe, /push/unsubscribe, /push/test.
Safety: endpoints must be on Google/Mozilla/Apple/Microsoft push services (no server-side
requests to arbitrary addresses); dead phones are removed automatically.
