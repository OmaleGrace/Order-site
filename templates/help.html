Frequently asked questions

Why is my coupon not working?

Check that the coupon is active, within its start/expiry window, and that your food subtotal meets its minimum order requirement.

How do loyalty points work?

Completed food spend earns points according to ENIE's loyalty rules. During checkout, eligible points can reduce the food amount; delivery fees are separate.

Can I change a scheduled order?

Open your order and contact support if you need help with a scheduled order.

Can I contact a shop?

Use Messages from a shop page to contact the vendor directly about an order or shop question.
