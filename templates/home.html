Questions

Straight answers.

How do I know a shop is real?

Shops go through an application where we speak to the owner and check their ID before the shop opens. Shops with a ✔ Verified badge have passed that check, and each shop page shows how many orders it has completed.

How do I pay?

At checkout you pay online through Paystack. Your order shows as paid once Paystack confirms the payment.

Can I order from two shops at once?

Not yet. Each order is from one shop, so it can go to one kitchen. Check out, then start a new order for the next shop.

Pickup or delivery?

Each shop chooses what it offers. At checkout you see their options and any delivery fee before you pay.

How much does it cost to sell here?

A one-time setup fee of ₦{{naira .SetupFeeKobo}} and no monthly fee. On each sale we keep {{.CommissionPercent}}% of the food total (delivery fees are not included) and the rest is yours. You pay the setup fee only after your application is approved.

How do I become a vendor?

Create an account, then apply from the Sell with us page. We will call you to verify your identity. Once you are approved, you pay the setup fee and your shop opens.
